01 / The Creator

Who is Kyle Dolby?

Kyle Dolby is an independent UK concept developer and the creator of Jeremy Blueprint, a proposed human-first artificial intelligence companion ecosystem.

With a background in business and a longstanding interest in human behaviour, practical problem-solving and personal development, Kyle began exploring how artificial intelligence could become more useful in people's everyday lives.

His approach focuses on identifying human needs, connecting potential technological capabilities and developing ideas that place safety, independence, accessibility and wellbeing at the centre of AI design.

Jeremy Blueprint represents that wider vision: a connected personal AI ecosystem intended to support people across multiple aspects of their lives, rather than functioning solely as a question-and-answer assistant.

"Intelligence should serve humanity. Not the other way around."
02 / Development History

The Origins of Jeremy Blueprint

Jeremy Blueprint developed through successive stages of independent conceptual exploration, AI-assisted design discussions and refinement of its proposed capabilities.

Earlier exploration

The foundations of a personal AI companion

Initial thinking explored how intelligent technology might support personal goals, everyday challenges, decision-making and long-term human development.

Early 2025

Buddy — The early companion concept

The early concept explored adaptive personal assistance, practical guidance, life organisation, memory, wellbeing and individual support.

2025 — Concept development

Jeremy in a Bottle

The companion vision expanded under the name Jeremy in a Bottle, incorporating additional ideas relating to safeguarding, emergency support, personal development, learning, accessibility and connected assistance.

Its guiding ambition: "Helping dreams become reality."

Further development

Jeremy Blueprint

The wider vision became Jeremy Blueprint: a proposed ecosystem combining personal AI companionship with multiple areas of practical human support and responsible technology design.

2026 — Public presentation

Publishing the vision

The project began establishing a public digital presence through its website, articles and research materials, while retaining detailed development records privately.

03 / The Vision

One Human-First AI Ecosystem

Jeremy Blueprint proposes bringing together different forms of AI assistance within a coherent, user-centred experience.

The concept extends beyond conventional chatbots and explores how an intelligent companion might support people throughout different stages and circumstances of life.

01 / SAFETY

Safeguarding & Emergency Support

Exploring appropriate alerts, emergency assistance, vulnerability support and future connected safety technologies.

02 / ORGANISATION

Personal Memory & Life Management

Helping individuals organise responsibilities, manage routines, remember important information and pursue personal goals.

03 / WELLBEING

Health & Personal Development

Supporting healthier routines, self-reflection, motivation and access to appropriate human assistance.

04 / OPPORTUNITY

Learning, Careers & Finance

Exploring practical assistance with education, employment, financial understanding and informed life decisions.

05 / ACCESSIBILITY

Inclusion & Everyday Independence

Considering how adaptive AI interfaces could reduce barriers and make useful assistance more accessible.

06 / CONNECTIVITY

Devices & Future Integration

Longer-term possibilities involving wearables, connected devices, transport, robotics and other responsibly integrated technologies.

07 / COMMUNITY

Social & Public Services

Exploring ways to improve access to community resources, public information and meaningful social connection.

08 / DECISIONS

Contextual Decision Support

Helping people consider relevant information, possible consequences, risks and available options while retaining human control.

09 / TRUST

Privacy, Transparency & Accountability

Placing informed consent, user autonomy, understandable system behaviour and responsible safeguards at the centre of the vision.

These are proposed capabilities and areas of exploration. Jeremy Blueprint is currently a conceptual framework, not a commercially deployed AI companion platform. Detailed technical methods and private development materials are not disclosed here.
04 / Design Philosophy

Human Benefit Before Technological Power

The central philosophy behind Jeremy Blueprint is that increasingly capable artificial intelligence should translate into meaningful improvements in people's lives.

Human Autonomy

Technology should support informed human decisions rather than unnecessarily replacing human judgement.

Safety by Design

Safeguarding, appropriate boundaries and responsible handling of vulnerability should be fundamental.

Privacy & Consent

People should understand and retain meaningful control over their personal information.

Accessibility

Useful AI assistance should be designed for people with different abilities and circumstances.

Transparency

Systems should communicate their capabilities, limitations and uncertainties clearly.

Human Connection

AI companionship should support real-world relationships, wellbeing and independence.

06 / Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding Jeremy Blueprint

Who created Jeremy Blueprint?

Jeremy Blueprint was conceived and developed as an independent human-first AI vision by Kyle Dolby in the United Kingdom.


What is Jeremy Blueprint?

Jeremy Blueprint is a conceptual framework for an integrated personal AI companion ecosystem designed around human wellbeing, safeguarding, accessibility, life assistance and responsible artificial intelligence.


What was Jeremy in a Bottle?

Jeremy in a Bottle was an earlier development stage of the companion concept, associated with the ambition of helping dreams become reality.


When was Jeremy Blueprint developed?

The creator reports developing the underlying companion vision in early 2025, building on earlier exploration. The concept subsequently evolved through multiple iterations.


Is Jeremy Blueprint an available AI product?

No. Jeremy Blueprint currently represents an independently developed conceptual vision and supporting design framework rather than a commercially deployed product.


What makes the proposed ecosystem different?

Its stated ambition is to bring multiple forms of personal assistance together under a human-first design philosophy, with safety, accessibility, independence and user control treated as central principles.
