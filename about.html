01 / The Creator

Who is Kyle Dolby?

Kyle Dolby is an independent UK concept developer and creator of Jeremy Blueprint, a proposed human-first artificial intelligence companion ecosystem.

With a background in business and an interest in human behaviour, practical problem-solving and personal development, Kyle began exploring how AI could become more useful in everyday life.

His approach focuses on understanding human needs, identifying opportunities for technological assistance and developing a connected vision that places safety, accessibility, autonomy and wellbeing at its centre.

Rather than viewing AI solely as a tool for answering questions, Jeremy Blueprint explores how intelligent technology might provide meaningful support across different aspects of a person's life.

"Intelligence should serve humanity. Not the other way around."
02 / Origins & Development

From Buddy to Jeremy in a Bottle

Jeremy Blueprint developed through successive stages of independent conceptual exploration, AI-assisted design discussions and refinement of a wider personal companion vision.

Earlier exploration

The foundations

Initial thinking explored how AI might help people manage challenges, pursue ambitions, make informed decisions and improve their everyday circumstances.

Early 2025

Buddy

The early companion concept explored personal organisation, contextual guidance, memory, routines, motivation and adaptive everyday assistance.

2025 — Continued development

Jeremy in a Bottle

The companion concept expanded to explore additional capabilities involving safeguarding, wellbeing, emergency support, accessibility, learning, personal development and connected assistance.

Its guiding ambition: "Helping dreams become reality."

Wider ecosystem development

Jeremy Blueprint

The vision developed into a broader framework exploring how multiple forms of personal AI assistance could work together within a responsible human-first ecosystem.

2026 — Public presentation

Sharing the vision

The project established a public presence through its website, articles and research materials, while retaining more detailed development documentation privately.

Development history: The creator reports original concept development in early 2025, building on earlier exploration, with proposal activity during June 2025. The complete historical development archive is not reproduced publicly. Original documents, versions and dates should be assessed from their underlying records where precise provenance is required.

04 / Design Principles

Human Benefit Before Technological Power

The central philosophy behind Jeremy Blueprint is that advances in artificial intelligence should translate into meaningful improvements in people's lives.

Human Autonomy

AI should strengthen informed human judgement and independence.

Safety by Design

Safeguarding and responsible boundaries should be fundamental.

Privacy & Consent

People should understand and control how personal information is used.

Accessibility

Useful assistance should be available to people with different needs and abilities.

Transparency

Systems should communicate capabilities, limitations and uncertainty clearly.

Human Connection

AI should support real-world relationships, wellbeing and personal independence.
