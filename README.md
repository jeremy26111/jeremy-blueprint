## About Jeremy Blueprint

Jeremy Blueprint is an independent human-first artificial intelligence ecosystem concept developed by Kyle Dolby.

The vision is to create a personal AI companion that supports individuals throughout everyday life while prioritising human autonomy, safety, accessibility, privacy and transparency.

The project evolved through three stages:

- Buddy

- Jeremy in a Bottle

- Jeremy Blueprint

Development began in early 2025, with proposals and correspondence dating to June 2025, according to the creator's records.

## Ecosystem Vision

The proposed ecosystem includes:

- Personal AI companionship

- Safety and safeguarding

- Organisation and memory

- Health and wellbeing

- Education, careers and learning

- Financial planning and support

- Accessibility and inclusion

- Community and public services

- Transport and connected devices

- Future robotics and automation

The broader vision is to make AI more accessible, practical and beneficial to humanity.

## Project Status

Jeremy Blueprint is currently an independently developed conceptual design, not a commercially launched or technically validated product.

The website contains published articles, project information and selected supporting records.
