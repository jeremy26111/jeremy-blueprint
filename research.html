Jeremy Blueprint

A Human-First AI Companion Ecosystem

Independent concept and vision by Kyle Dolby
Original development: Early 2025, as reported by the creator

Return to Main Website About Kyle Dolby

1. Executive Overview

Jeremy Blueprint presents a vision for an interconnected artificial intelligence companion designed around human needs, independence and long-term wellbeing.

Rather than treating artificial intelligence as an isolated chatbot, the framework proposes a connected ecosystem capable of supporting people throughout everyday life.

Its guiding principle is that intelligence should serve humanity, not require humanity to adapt to it.

2. Origins and Development

The concept developed through earlier explorations known as Buddy and Jeremy in a Bottle during early 2025.

It subsequently evolved into Jeremy Blueprint, a broader human-first artificial intelligence vision.

The creator maintains historical development records and correspondence relating to the concept. Independent verification of the complete development chronology has not been published here.

4. The Proposed Companion Ecosystem

Jeremy Blueprint proposes connected support across multiple areas of human life.

  • Safety, safeguarding and emergency assistance
  • Organisation, memory and daily planning
  • Wellbeing and responsible companionship
  • Learning, careers and personal development
  • Accessibility and independent living
  • Financial and legal information navigation
  • Healthcare and public-service navigation
  • Community and social connection
  • Transport, devices and future robotics integration
  • News, alerts and contextual information

These are proposed capabilities rather than claims of a fully implemented product.

The wider public ecosystem overview identifies additional areas of conceptual development, including decision support, personalisation, community participation and connected services.

5. Safeguarding and Human Independence

The framework places human safety and independent decision-making at the centre of AI companionship.

Proposed safeguards include proactive risk awareness, emergency assistance, appropriate human oversight and clear boundaries for sensitive situations.

Any real-world implementation would require technical validation, privacy safeguards, appropriate professional oversight and compliance with applicable law.

6. Personal Memory and Continuity

Jeremy Blueprint envisions a companion that can support long-term goals, personal organisation and continuity across everyday activities.

Users should remain informed about what information is retained and have meaningful control over personal information.

7. Accessibility and Inclusion

The vision includes support for people with different abilities, circumstances and accessibility requirements.

The objective is to expand independence and access to opportunity rather than create unnecessary dependence on technology.

8. Research and Development Priorities

  • Privacy-preserving personal memory
  • Safeguarding and emergency-response validation
  • Human oversight and professional boundaries
  • Accessibility and inclusive interface design
  • Secure interoperability with external services
  • Long-term wellbeing and autonomy evaluation
  • Technical feasibility and prototype development

9. Current Development Status

Jeremy Blueprint is an independently documented conceptual framework and proposed ecosystem.

This publication does not claim a deployed, commercially validated AI platform or a completed technical implementation.

Future progress would require engineering, research, safety testing, partnerships and independent evaluation.
